## Experience
Gentle opening camera approach; drag rotation with damping; service hotspots; cinematic interruptible camera journeys; reset to overview. Selected siding supports a draggable before/after comparison on the model with identical camera perspective. Other services have working focus states and descriptive panels, with additional transformations explicitly identified as prototype scope when absent. A mobile service selector and keyboard controls offer alternatives to precision hotspot use. Reduced motion disables automatic travel. Include a usable no-WebGL fallback.
